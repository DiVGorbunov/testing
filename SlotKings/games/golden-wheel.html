About the Game

Golden Wheel of Kings is our flagship live game show — a towering 54-sector wheel wrapped in gold and bathed in royal purple light.

Players simply pick a number and watch the wheel decide. The Crown Bonus adds a second act: whenever the pointer lands on a golden sector, a bonus spin multiplies the next result, chaining up to x500.

With one-tap betting, autoplay and portrait-first mobile design, it's the easiest entry point into live gaming your lobby can offer.
