About the Game

Crown of Pharaoh: Hold & Win blends the timeless charm of Egyptian-themed slots with modern, punchy pacing. Hieroglyph royals, ankh scatters and radiant sun coins fill 5x3 reels rendered in deep purple dusk and shimmering gold.

The heart of the game is the Hold & Win bonus: six or more sun coins freeze the board and award three respins. Every new coin resets the counter, and filling all fifteen positions awards the Grand Jackpot of x2000 total bet.

Tuned for high volatility and a 96.2% RTP, the game keeps sessions dramatic while frequent scarab wilds keep the base game alive between bonuses.
